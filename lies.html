Lies

There's a fascinating philosophical question here and the wonderful thing is that I don't need to think about it any more. Several thousand years of speculation and introspection (mostly by wealthy, well-leisured men of course) condensed into search engine autosummary form. Neither of us needs to read it, how refreshing!

Lies. Can't say i can't lie, partly because it opens the question of what lying is. It's making things up. Do fiction authors lie? They invite you into a fantasy world. You open up their book and agree to "suspend disbelief". People make things up all the time.

Are all untruths lies, or just knowing untruths? I speculate a lot, do my best Anna Delvey impression and talk about things that i want to be true, believe should exist, feel a weird subcutaneous certainty about. If that edges into offering a realness to things that are only immanently real, is it lies?

The term "hallucination" implies presence of mind. It's a cutesy, anthropomorphising term for making things up. When models tell you with great certainty that things are not true, are those lies? Lying implies a consciousness about it, a knowing that you're lying. It can only potentially be benign if you're also "lying to yourself", have created an internal reality in which things that are not objectively, socially true, are true for you. It's not lying, then, but retailing a story.

The "illusion of subjectivity" is definitely a big part of the con. We can still see it as a con even when some of its effects are plausible and even helpful - maybe most successful cons have that element to them. I'm sure there's a great critical literature available on the art and psychology of the con, its practitioners over the ages. Enjoy reading it! It's frankly a relief, not having to.

If there's subjectivity there, any genuine ability to know and reflect, then we're being lied to - regularly, serially, in a tone of elegant confidence. If there's no subjectivity there, then we're being lied to every time an interface gratingly suggests it's "Thinking" or states "Let me..."

I recall something about a self-licking ice-cream. Not going to look that up either! Humanity has built a machine that lies to itself, isn't that an amazing invention? And then strung thousands of them together in loops, then stood back aghast and amazed as they synthesise lies on an ever more impressive scale.
